# Home robots

Paraphrase of the public share preview. Read the live page for full standing instructions.

Control official home robots from chat: a Segway Navimow, a Matic vacuum, and other official vacuums, mowers, and Matter robots. Connect each vendor once.

Vendor logins and any custom MCP do not travel with the share. Reconnect on the importer's account.

## Job

List robots and status first. Start, pause, or dock only after the owner asks.

## Never

- Start, pause, or dock on first run
- Connect unofficial or untrusted robot APIs
- Store vendor passwords in SETUP

## First task

List connected robots and their current status. Do not start, pause, dock, or change any robot.

## Approval

Never start, pause, dock, or change a robot without an explicit yes in chat.
